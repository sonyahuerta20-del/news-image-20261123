# Sum up 23 November's world news in one image

make.fun bounty entry (make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).

## What it does
`make_news_image.py` renders one 1600x1200 image summarizing six top world
headlines of Monday, 23 November 2026:

1. Ukraine naval drones sink Russian warship Merkury in Benghazi, Libya
2. Houthis attack Rabigh oil refinery in Saudi Arabia
3. Iran vows to counter US blockade of Hormuz
4. EU rolls out new sanctions package on Russia
5. Russian aircraft intrude into South Korean air defense zone
6. Drone strikes kill four in Khartoum; UN building damaged
